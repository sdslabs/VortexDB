# Draft Proposal: Northstar Energy Enterprise Upgrade

**Drafted:** 2026-01-20  
**Status:** Superseded — do not use for current pricing

This draft proposed an Enterprise upgrade for Northstar’s 42 sites. It described a 60-day raw telemetry retention period for Growth and listed webhook delivery as a paid add-on.

The proposal was never signed. The plan catalogue changed on 2026-04-01: Growth now includes 90 days of raw telemetry, while webhooks are included for Growth and Enterprise. For current terms, use `pricing-and-plans.md` and the customer’s signed agreement.

This document is retained only to demonstrate how retrieval systems should recognize stale commercial information.
